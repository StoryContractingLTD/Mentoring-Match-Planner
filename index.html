Mentoring & Coaching Match Planner

Load the responses from the Mentoring & Coaching forms. The planner reads each person's match code from Find Your Match, scores every possible pairing and suggests an allocation that respects each mentor's or coach's capacity. Suggestions are a starting point. Always sense-check for line management, existing relationships and anything the form tells you.

1. Load responses

Upload the Excel export from MS Forms (or a CSV), or paste rows straight from Excel. You can load the supporter form and the request form together; the code tells the planner who's who. Columns headed Name, Email and Line manager are picked up automatically. The line manager column is used to stop people being matched with their own manager.

Privacy: everything happens in this browser window. Nothing is uploaded or saved, and closing the tab clears the data. Export your allocation before you close.
